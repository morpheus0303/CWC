What we need from you

A few decisions and a few files

Nothing here is final until you say so. These answers let us finish the design and start building.

  1. Approve the palette and fonts, or tell us what to change.
  2. Pick a homepage direction. We recommend Version 2.
  3. Dr. Bertha Campo: a portrait, a short bio, and her title exactly as it should appear.
  4. Photos of Dr. Valdes, the team and the office, or a date for a short photo session.
  5. A vector logo (SVG, PDF or AI) from the original designer, for print and signage.
  6. Translation reviewers for Spanish, Russian and French: native speakers on the team, or a service.
