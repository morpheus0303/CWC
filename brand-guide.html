One logo, one palette and one type system, used the same way everywhere.

Prepared for the Collaborative Women's Care team · September 2026

WHY THIS GUIDE

Keep the logo. Bring everything else into line with it.

Today the website, social posts and printed pieces each look a little different. This guide keeps the seal exactly as it is and builds a warmer, more consistent brand around its two blues and its leaf shapes.

Keep

The seal, its navy and sky-blue inks, and the site's trusted button blue.

Consolidate

Three unrelated fonts become one system; scattered grays and blues become eight named colors.

Add

Warmth: a cream background and one blush accent, so the brand feels caring, not clinical.

04

LOGO

Give it room, and never alter it

Clear space

One ring-letter height on every side (the dashed line). No text or edges inside it.

Minimum size

96 px wide on screen, 1 inch in print. Smaller, the ring lettering blurs.

Don't

  • Recolor it, or set it in a single color
  • Stretch, squash, rotate or crop into the ring
  • Add shadows, outlines, glows or gradients
  • Place it on photos, mid-tone colors or navy without the cream circle
  • Retype the practice name beside it in another font

TYPOGRAPHY · OPTIONS TO CONSIDER

Our pick, and two alternates

A · Selected

For you. With you.

Cormorant Garamond + Nunito Sans. Elegant and personal; the thin strokes match the logo's line-art.

B · Warm classic

For you. With you.

Lora + Nunito Sans. Sturdier serif that holds up better at small sizes and on print.

C · Modern

For you. With you.

Josefin Sans + Nunito Sans. All sans-serif, closest to the logo's lettering; cleaner but less warm.

Body text is Nunito Sans in every option, so only the headlines change.
